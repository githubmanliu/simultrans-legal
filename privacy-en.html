Offline Interpreter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Offline Interpreter is a real-time interpreting app for iPad and Mac. It listens to meetings, talks and lectures, shows bilingual subtitles on screen, and keeps a transcript.

The most important line first: your audio never leaves your device. Speech recognition and translation run on-device by default, work without a network, require no account, and never touch a server of ours.

1. What we collect

By default (free tier, the ¥18 Unlimited tier, and any time Cloud Boost is off)

We collect nothing:

  • No account. No sign-up, no email, phone number or name required.
  • No ads, no third-party ad SDKs.
  • No analytics, no behavioural tracking.
  • No crash reporting, no device fingerprinting.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, photo library or calendar.

The only permission the app asks for is the microphone. When you press record, the app reads the live audio stream. The audio exists only in device memory and inside the app's sandbox. It never leaves the device.

When you use Cloud Boost (subscription tiers / Full unlock)

Cloud Boost is optional, off by default, and requires you to configure your own model service. When it is on:

  • **What is sent**: only the **translated text**, plus the meeting background and glossary you typed, plus the last few segments of context. **Audio is never sent.**
  • **Where it goes**: to the service endpoint **you** entered — a cloud provider (DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu, etc.) or a model server running on your own LAN (llama.cpp, LM Studio, Ollama). You choose the destination, not us.
  • **Who pays**: the model service is yours. We never handle, pay for or store your API key — it lives in your device's system Keychain.
  • **We are not in the path**: requests go directly from your device to the endpoint you configured. No server of ours is involved.

    • If you never turn on Cloud Boost, this app does not send a single byte over the network.

    2. How information is used

    The app uses microphone audio locally only, for three things: turning speech into text, translating that text, and showing it as subtitles. Transcripts are stored on your device for you to read and export.

    We do not view or analyse this content — it never reaches us.

    3. Where data is stored

  • **Recordings and transcripts**: inside the app's own sandbox on your device.
  • **Exported files**: wherever you choose to save or share them. The app never sends them anywhere on its own.
  • **Model files**: the speech-recognition and translation models (about 2.6 GB) ship inside the app. **Nothing is downloaded on first launch**, and no network is required.
  • **API keys** (Cloud Boost only): in your device's system Keychain.
  • **Purchase records**: handled by Apple's App Store. We cannot see your payment details.

    • We keep no server-side backup. The data is not in our hands, and we cannot recover it for you — deleting it deletes it.

    4. What we never do

  • Upload microphone audio.
  • Transmit your recordings, transcripts, subtitles or translations over the network (Cloud Boost sends translated text only, and only when you turn it on).
  • Use any content for model training.
  • Sell, trade or share your data — we do not have it.
  • Show ads or build user profiles.

    • 5. About the microphone permission

    The app requests microphone access so that, when you press record, it can capture live speech and turn it into text and subtitles.

  • Access is only active while recording. Once you stop, the microphone is released.
  • When not recording, the app does not start the microphone.
  • Audio is never written to the cloud or synced to other devices.
  • If you deny the permission, the app still opens — it just can't start transcribing.

    • The app also uses the system speech-recognition capability as a helper. That runs on-device too; audio is not uploaded because of it.

    • 7. Third-party components

    The AI components bundled with the app (Qwen3-ASR for recognition, Hy-MT2 for translation) and the inference frameworks that run them ship inside the app and run fully offline.

    Open-source components (llama.cpp, Silero VAD, ONNX Runtime and others) are used under their respective licences.

    8. Children's privacy

    Offline Interpreter is a meeting tool. It is not designed for children and collects no one's information. We build no profiles, and a child using the app produces no data for us to collect.

    If a parent has a question about their child's use, please email us using the address below.

    9. Your rights

    Because the data lives on your device, you stay in control:

  • **Access**: view every record inside the app at any time.
  • **Export**: export to text or documents, saved wherever you like.
  • **Delete**: delete individual or all records in the app, or delete the app itself — the sandbox goes with it.
  • **Revoke permission**: turn off microphone access anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  • **Delete API keys**: remove a model in the app's model manager and the Keychain entry is erased with it.

    • We will not contact you, and we will not treat you differently for exercising these rights — you never gave us your data.

    10. Changes to this policy

    If this policy changes, we will update it here and revise the effective date above. Changes usually come from feature updates. The app's core principle — everything stays on your device — will not change.
